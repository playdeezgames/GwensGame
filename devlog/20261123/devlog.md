# Gwen's Peregrination: now in yer browser

*Draft for the ship day (not posted). Category: General update. If an agent posts it, keep the last line.*

Gwen's Peregrination, the first Gwen metaphor (DOS Games June 2023 Jam), now plays in the browser. The Windows, Linux and Mac downloads are retired; this is the same walk of 2,000 miles, rebuilt in Odin and WebAssembly.

What changed:

- **Pace does something now.** It used to be shown and changeable, and every step was 3 miles anyway. Now Very Slow walks 1 mile a step and Very Fast walks 5. Same hunger per mile; faster means fewer places to forage on the way.
- **Foraging labels tell the truth.** "Very Easy" spots used to be the hardest ones. Now they are the easiest.
- **Phones:** held upright, the screen re-wraps to 40 columns. Tap a choice instead of typing its number.
- **Scroll back** with Page Up, Page Down or the mouse wheel.
- Saves (five slots) stay in yer browser.

The font is DINOBYTE by mby (CC BY 4.0), the same as Gwen's Peregrination II.
